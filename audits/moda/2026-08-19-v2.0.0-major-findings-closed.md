## What this changes, and what it doesn't

- `moda.yaml`'s `adoption.relationship`: `audited_against` → `conforms_to`, effective at the v2.0.0 tag.
- `conformance/moda.yaml`'s `assessment.result` **stays `partial`**, not `full`. The audit's `minor` findings (3 items) and `observation`s (4 items) remain open — none was a condition for `conforms_to`, but they're still real, honestly declared gaps, not resolved by this note. Conflating "the relationship criterion is met" with "every control is satisfied" would misstate what actually changed.
- No individual control status in `conformance/moda.yaml` is being rewritten by this note — those already reflect the current state of each control, independent of the `relationship` label. This note only records that the specific, narrower condition the frozen audit set for `relationship` has now been met.

## Why a new file, not an edit to the frozen audit

`audits/moda/2026-08-17-v1.0.0-self-audit.md` is immutable evidence of what was true on 2026-08-17, per its own stated convention (same principle as invariant 3 — a document's history isn't overwritten to reflect a later state). This note is dated separately and cross-references it rather than modifying it.
