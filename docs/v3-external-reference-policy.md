# V3.0.0 contract — external references

**Status:** published in V3.0.0.

OKF, KSoR, Agent Knowledge and similar materials may be cited in sections
labelled `comparison`, `inspiration`, `research` or `context`. The citation
does not make the external material a Hipocampo authority, dependency,
protocol or adopted format.

Adoption of an external concept, format or dependency requires a separate
analysis of compatibility, attribution, operational impact and a **new Decision Record**.
Examples and comparisons must not claim compatibility that has not
been validated.
