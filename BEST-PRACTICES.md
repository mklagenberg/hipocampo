# Best practices for Hipocampo V3

Use the verified version-matched SPEC and target vault's additive restrictions.
The [earlier guide](BEST-PRACTICES-V2.md) remains historical reference.

- Begin with a bounded request, entity/vault/policy and actual authorization.
- Preserve source/body/Unicode, versions, relations, Artifacts and limitations.
- Keep processing, maturity, epistemic nature, staleness and privacy separate.
- Organize logical Collections with overlapping membership; a folder/tag does
  not confer acceptance, authority or access.
- Read the minimum needed: metadata, envelope, selected Chunk with parent
  context; explicit permission before wider expansion.
- Treat proposals as proposals. AI review is not approval or factual proof.
- Every knowledge mutation passes through canonical CRUD. Metadata capabilities
  are separate and cannot become a bypass for Record bodies or writes.
- Never move personal/restricted content into a public methodology or management.
- Validate context and prior effects before resuming a checkpoint; contain item
  failures and preserve the pending queue instead of silently dropping them.
- Use progressive privacy remediation; adoption does not authorize a full sweep.
- Keep package/update checks, installation, publication and vault migration as
  separate decisions. A declared release target is not evidence of its existence.

[release proof boundaries](docs/v3-release-preparation.md) and
[migration execution](docs/v3-migration-execution.md).
